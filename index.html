How to Play?

Build the rainbow before your opponent!

1 Split

3 1 2 5

The Dealer splits 4 unicorns into 2 pairs.

2 Pick

3 1 👈 2 5

The Receiver picks a pair. The Dealer gets the other.

3 Make 7

1 + 6 2 + 5 3 + 4

Pair your unicorns whose values add up to 7.

4 Make a Fragment

3 + 4 →

Choose one unicorn to become a Rainbow Fragment; the other is discarded. Pick a color you already own and you draw a fresh unicorn instead.

5 Complete the Rainbow

Collect all 7 colors to win!
